ABOUT US

Our Mission

Hope for Humanity exists to help people affected by homelessness and poverty and to create practical pathways toward dignity, stability and opportunity.

Our work includes food relief, mobile soup kitchens, portable shower facilities for homeless people, community support, education, bursaries, sponsorship initiatives and projects that help impoverished communities become stronger and more sustainable.
