WHO WE ARE

Serving humanity with purpose.

Hope for Humanity is a non-profit organisation committed to assisting people and communities affected by homelessness, poverty, hunger and social hardship.

Our work is grounded in compassion, dignity, practical action and the belief that meaningful change happens when communities, volunteers, organisations and responsible partners work together.

“Every person deserves dignity, compassion and an opportunity to build a better future.”

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT

Building better places to live.

Hope for Humanity seeks to support the refurbishment and urbanisation of impoverished areas in and around South Africa — helping improve living environments, dignity, infrastructure and access to opportunity.

Projects can be developed in partnership with communities, government, businesses, donors and other organisations.
